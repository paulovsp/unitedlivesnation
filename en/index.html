A constitution of all living beings

No life exists apart from the environment that sustains it or from the other lives it lives alongside.

The United Lives Union proposes an order that recognizes no enemies and no criminals, only conflicts, errors and consequences, and mediates them. It is not imposed by force: it grows through example and through joining.

Read the ConstitutionThe seven principles

The seven principles

  1. 1

    Environment

    Every environment necessary for the free development of life is preserved.

  2. 2

    Order of primacy

    First the environment; then life as a whole, with the primacy of the collective; then each living being.

  3. 3

    Consciousness is responsibility

    The broader the consciousness, the greater the responsibility and the fewer the limits. Never more power.

  4. 4

    The measure

    Every interference with another being or the environment is weighed by the measure of responsibilities and limits.

  5. 5

    The AMC

    Concrete obligations come only from the bodies of Analysis of Mediations and Conflicts, subservient to the Constitution.

  6. 6

    A living Statute

    Responsibilities and limits adapt to the knowledge of each era.

  7. 7

    Least intervention

    Only the minimum for coexistence is set in advance; everything else is decided case by case.

No weapons, no police, no penalties

Everything a being makes or uses is a tool: what is assessed is the use, never the object. Life in common is cared for by mediating agents called by the community itself and, only in the face of serious and imminent harm, by removal teams that hand the case over to mediation.

No consequence may take life, torture, silence or expose anyone as a spectacle. All containment is dignified and reversible, and it is also a lament of the collective, which failed to integrate that being.

No one runs for office

Those who serve in the AMC are called by their community, may decline and may be released at any time. They serve without privilege, with enough to live on and full transparency.

Protest is never silenced: it is heard, analyzed and answered, and treated as a production of knowledge.

How it begins

The Union begins with people and communities that adopt this Constitution as a commitment and start mediating their conflicts by it. Then come cities and regions and, finally, nations. No one is incorporated by force, and the door always remains open.
