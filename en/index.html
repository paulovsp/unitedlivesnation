A movement for a constituent revolution

We are one life among lives. It is time the law knew it.

The United Lives Nation is a movement for a law that protects the environment, life and every living being from human excess. It is not imposed by force: it grows through example.

Read the manifestoRead the Constitution

Humanity has fallen under its own spell

Every achievement increased our power to transform the world and, with it, the belief that the world exists for us. From this species narcissism comes overharm: harm beyond what is necessary, excess, waste.

The devastation of environments, war and inequality look like separate crises, but they grow from the same root: whoever learns to place themselves above life learns to place themselves above other humans. And since the first codes, the law has protected the owner, inheritance and hierarchy, and treated other beings as things.

Areas of focus

The most necessary constituent revolution of our time is to establish this legislation itself, with one priority: to stop overharm, the excessive and unnecessary destruction of the environment, of life as a whole and of every other living being.

  1. 1

    Interspeciesism

    Bringing all living beings, of every species, under the protection of the law.

  2. 2

    Conflict with the least harm

    Accepting conflict as part of life and resolving it with the least possible harm, through mediation rather than punishment.

  3. 3

    A legislation that guarantees protection

    The law exists to secure this right. From it, the free development of the environment, of life as a whole and of each living being is protected.

How it begins

The revolution begins where life happens: in the building where neighbors do not know each other, on the street, in the neighborhood, in the village. It begins when a community gathers, adopts this proposal as a commitment and starts mediating its conflicts by it. Then come cities, regions, nations.

No one is incorporated by force, and the door always remains open.
